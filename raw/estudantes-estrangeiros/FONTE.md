# Fonte — estudantes do ensino superior vindos do estrangeiro (mobilidade de grau), Portugal

- Eurostat, UOE (UNESCO-OCDE-Eurostat) — `educ_uoe_mobs02` «Mobile students from abroad enrolled by education level, sex and country of origin» (atualizado a 2026-11-18); filtro geo=PT, isced11=ED5-8 (ensino superior, níveis 5 a 8), sex=T, unidade: número.
  API: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/educ_uoe_mobs02?format=JSON&lang=EN&geo=PT&isced11=ED5-8&sex=T — descarregado em 2026-11-20, sem alterações.
- Eurostat — `educ_uoe_mobs03` «Share of mobile students from abroad enrolled by education level, sex and country of origin» (percentagem do total de inscritos), partner=WRL_X_REP.
  API: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/educ_uoe_mobs03?format=JSON&lang=EN&geo=PT&isced11=ED5-8&sex=T&partner=WRL_X_REP — 2026-11-20.
- Eurostat — `educ_uoe_enrt01` «Students enrolled in tertiary education by education level, programme orientation, sex, type of institution and intensity of participation» (total de inscritos, para controlo da percentagem).
  API: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/educ_uoe_enrt01?format=JSON&lang=EN&geo=PT&isced11=ED5-8&sex=T — 2026-11-20.
